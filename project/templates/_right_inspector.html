Parameters ? This panel summarizes the selected sample, including encapsulation efficiency, ATR-derived ratio, and crystalline versus amorphous fractions.
Material composition ? Shows the relative contribution of the identified crystalline phases for the selected sample. Top phases are the dominant assigned phases in that sample.
ATR-IR spectrum ? ATR-IR is an infrared spectrum of the solid sample measured in attenuated total reflectance mode. The ATR-IR bands ratio compares the protein Amide I band (≈1647 cm⁻¹) with the ZIF framework Zn–N stretching band (≈421 cm⁻¹), giving an estimate of protein retained in the composite.

ATR-IR bands ratio: Amide I band (≈1647 cm⁻¹, protein) ÷ Zn–N stretching band (≈421 cm⁻¹, ZIF framework).

Repeated XRD experiments ? Powder X-ray diffraction (XRD) patterns reveal the crystalline phases present through characteristic diffraction peaks matched against known ZIF structures. Up to three repeated measurements are shown so you can assess the reproducibility of the phase assignment for this sample.
